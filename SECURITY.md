# Security Policy

Cgenerate values the responsible disclosure of security and privacy concerns.

## Reporting a Security Issue

Please do not publicly disclose suspected security vulnerabilities through GitHub issues, discussions, or social media.

Report security concerns privately through the official Cgenerate contact page:

https://cgenerate.com/contact/

When submitting a report, please include:

- A clear description of the suspected issue
- The affected page, tool, or feature
- Steps required to reproduce the issue
- Relevant screenshots or supporting details
- The potential impact, if known

Please do not include passwords, payment information, private certificate files, or unnecessary personal information in your report.

## Responsible Disclosure

Please allow Cgenerate a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address a legitimate security concern before sharing information publicly.

Avoid actions that could:

- Access or modify another user's information
- Disrupt the availability of the service
- Damage data, systems, or infrastructure
- Violate applicable laws or third-party rights
- Expose sensitive information unnecessarily

## Privacy Note

Cgenerate processes certificate templates, recipient information, previews, and generated certificate files inside the user's browser session.

Uploaded templates and recipient files are not intentionally stored by Cgenerate on a server. Limited interface preferences may be stored locally in the user's browser.

For additional information, review the [Cgenerate Privacy Policy](https://cgenerate.com/privacy/).
